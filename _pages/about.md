I was born in Rome, where I earned my bachelor's and master's at [La Sapienza](https://www.uniroma1.it/en). My bachelor's thesis, supervised by Prof. Andrea Maselli, was on gravitational Love numbers, while my master's, with Prof. Fabio Riccioni, was on gravity from scattering amplitudes. Afterwards I moved to Barcelona for my Ph.D. at [ICE-CSIC](https://www.ice.csic.es) and the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona, under Prof. Sergei D. Odintsov, on different modified gravity models in cosmology, from inflation to dark energy. Alongside the cosmology, I study the tidal deformability of black holes and neutron stars immersed in matter and dark matter environments.
